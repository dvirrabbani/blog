I carry a notebook. Paper, not an app. This is not a productivity flex — it's closer to the opposite.

The point isn't to capture everything. It's that the act of writing something by hand is slow enough to make you decide whether it's worth writing at all. Most things aren't. That filter is the whole value.

## The rules I actually follow

1. One notebook at a time. No system, no index, no color coding.
2. Date at the top of the page. That's it.
3. If I haven't looked at a page in six months, it didn't matter.

The third rule is the liberating one. Almost nothing needs to be permanent.

## What I've noticed

Ideas I write down tend to come back better. Ideas I only think about tend to come back identical — the same loop, worn a little deeper each time. Writing seems to move something out of the queue and into a place where it can actually change.
