Every blog starts somewhere, and this one starts here.

I've been meaning to write things down for a while. Not because I think everything I have to say is worth saying, but because writing forces a kind of clarity that thinking alone never quite gets to.

## What this will be

Mostly notes to myself, published in the open. Things I figured out the hard way, books that stuck with me, occasional half-formed opinions I'll probably disagree with in a year.

## What this won't be

A content strategy. There's no posting schedule, no niche, no funnel. Just a place to put things.

Thanks for reading.
